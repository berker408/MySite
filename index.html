About个人简介

I am a Ph.D. student at Peking University, in the School of Advanced Manufacturing and Robotics.

My research focuses on robotic manipulation and skill transfer, with a particular interest in surgical robotics. I work on autonomous surgical manipulation, bringing together hierarchical planning, vision–language–action policies, and closed-loop anomaly management.

Previously, I studied Automation at Beihang University and conducted research on deep neural network training at LeapLab, Tsinghua University.

我是乔帆，目前在北京大学先进制造与机器人学院攻读博士学位。

我的研究聚焦机器人操作与技能迁移，重点关注手术机器人。目前参与自主手术操作系统研究，结合分层规划、视觉–语言–动作（VLA）策略与闭环异常管理。

此前，我在北京航空航天大学学习自动化，并曾在清华大学 LeapLab开展深度神经网络训练研究。
